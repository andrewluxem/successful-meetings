# Meeting audit scorecard

Score each recurring meeting 0 to 2 on each tenet. 0: absent. 1: partial. 2: consistently true. Maximum 8.

```
| Meeting | Cadence | Length | People | Owned and purposeful | Right sized | Participatory | Decisive | Score /8 | Verdict | The one change |
|---------|---------|--------|--------|----------------------|-------------|---------------|----------|----------|---------|----------------|
| [name]  | [weekly]| [30m]  | [6]    | [0-2]                | [0-2]       | [0-2]         | [0-2]    | [n]      | [see key] | [one sentence] |
```

## Verdict key

- **Keep** (7 to 8): leave it alone.
- **Fix** (5 to 6): keep it, apply the one change. Name exactly one; a meeting cannot absorb five fixes at once.
- **Shrink** (4 to 6 with size or length problems): same meeting, fewer people or fewer minutes.
- **Make async** (any score, if nothing requires live discussion): becomes a doc, a status update, or an email. Status readouts with no debate belong here.
- **Kill** (0 to 3): cancel it. If something breaks, that breakage will name the meeting that should replace it.

## Recovery line

End every audit with the recovery math, because hours are what make the verdicts land:

```
Applied verdicts return [X] person-hours per week ([attendees x minutes saved], across [N] meetings).
```
